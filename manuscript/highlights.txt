- Flood risk forecasts are recalibrated before they drive rescue resource allocation
- Conformal sets turn calibrated forecasts into demand ranges with stated coverage
- Routes slow with predicted flood depth and close where landslides block roads
- A trigger rule moves teams only when forecasts, risk classes or roads change
- Tested on the 2018 Kerala floods, with the 2019 floods as an independent test
